I am a Data Scientist at <a href="https://www.google.com" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="inline-company-link" title="Google"><img src="/images/Google_logo.svg" alt="Google" class="inline-company-logo inline-company-logo--google"></a>. Previously, I worked as a Data Scientist at <a href="https://web.archive.org/web/20230331120130/https://getcruise.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="inline-company-link" title="Cruise"><img src="/images/Cruise_logo.svg" alt="Cruise" class="inline-company-logo inline-company-logo--cruise"></a>, focusing on [autonomous vehicle safety](/work/#safety-simulation). I hold a [PhD in Biostatistics](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/school-of-public-health-fifth-anniversary-education) from the University of California, San Diego. I studied under the supervision of [Professor Armin Schwartzman](https://profiles.ucsd.edu/armin.schwartzman) on random field theory (RFT) based peak detection, and [Professor Steven Edland](https://profiles.ucsd.edu/steven.edland) on longitudinal data modeling. My research interests mainly lie in random field theory, longitudinal data analysis, clinical trial and experimental design, and causal inference. 

Beyond my work and research, I am always looking for opportunities to expand my skill set and stay up-to-date with the latest trends and technologies in statistics and data science. I enjoy using data to tell compelling stories, and making rational scientific judgments based on effective analysis. Please feel free to reach out to me if you are interested in my experience.
